Workshops

Bring a story to your group

Narrative games are collaborative storytelling. A Game Master guides a group through conflicts, from escaping a trap to saving the world, and everyone shapes how the story turns out.

Rolling the dice is only half of a turn. Win or lose, players describe what happens next, so everyone keeps thinking, talking and building on each other's ideas. In a world full of screens and scrolling, it is a chance to sit together and make something out of nothing but imagination.

Eshu Games runs sessions for schools, libraries, afterschool programs and community groups, adapted to any age, time limit or group size, in person or online.

Teamwork

Groups plan, decide and solve problems together.

Leadership

Players take charge of the story and lift each other up.

Creativity

Every success and every failure needs a new idea.

Wellbeing

Connection, confidence and a safe place to try things.

Sessions can weave in other subjects, with rewards inside the game for real-world work:

  • Writing
  • Math
  • Social studies
  • Coding
  • Music

World-building workshops use the same tools to imagine new places, histories and societies, the way every Eshu world began.

About

Eshu, keeper of the crossroads

In Yoruba tradition, Eshu is the trickster and messenger who stands where the roads meet, where every choice opens a new path.

Eshu Games is the studio of writer and game designer Ahmed K. Bey. It makes worlds you can enter more than one way: as a player, as a reader, or as part of a table telling the story together.
