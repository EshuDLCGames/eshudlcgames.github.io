Shattered Dreams

by Ahmed K. Bey

Shattered dreams forge nightmares that none can escape.
Running will only hasten your fate,
For time is relentless; it follows wherever you go.
Its speed not quicker nor slower, can it be slowed?
It is as fast as you perceive, and as slow as you believe,
So escape is not an option, for it will never leave.
The past is shattered glass
That is scattered beneath our feet,
Like rose petals of a wedding, or rice that no one eats.
They pave the road from yesterday to today, and on to the future.
Life is but a teacher; our perception is the tutor.
So learn what you can learn while learning is an option,
For the fruit that bears the seed of knowledge is eaten or gets rotten.
